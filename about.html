How JobNest Works

1

Companies subscribe

Employers register, choose a Premium, Gold or Platinum membership and get approved by an admin.

2

Jobs are posted

Companies publish vacancies with requirements, skills, salary range and a closing date.

3

Candidates apply

Job seekers search by keyword, category or location and apply with their CV in a few clicks.

4

Smart matching

The matching engine scores each application on skills, qualifications and experience to speed up shortlisting.
